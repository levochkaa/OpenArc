Getting Started

OpenArc keeps your tabs in a sidebar and your work in Spaces.

Tabs

New tabs go to Today and move to the Archive by themselves after a while. Pin a tab to keep it, or drag it up to Favorites to have it in every Space.

New tab or search
⌘T
Pin or unpin
⌘D
Archive a tab
⌘W
Clear Today
⇧⌘K
Next and previous tab
⌥⌘↓ ⌥⌘↑

Spaces

A Space has its own pinned tabs, Today and theme, and can use its own profile. Swipe sideways on the sidebar to change Space.

Next and previous Space
⌥⌘→ ⌥⌘←
Go to a Space
⌃1 ⌃2 …

Everything else

Hide the sidebar
⌘S
Split View
⌃⇧=
Small window
⌥⌘N
Library: downloads, media, archive
⇧⌘L
Capture part of a page
⇧⌘2

All shortcuts are in Settings, under Shortcuts. Help > Restore Data goes back to an earlier copy of your sidebar.
