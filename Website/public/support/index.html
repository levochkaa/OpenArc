OpenArc

Support

Write to support@openarc.su with questions, problems and requests about OpenArc for Mac, iPhone and iPad. Say which device and app version you use; Settings shows the version.

Sync

Sync is optional. Sign in from Account on iPad, from Settings on iPhone, or from Settings on Mac, with the same method on each device. Spaces and tabs sync; browsing history, passwords and website data stay on each device.

Deleting your account

Write to support@openarc.su from the email address of your account. We delete the account and everything synced with it within 30 days. Signing out keeps your Spaces and tabs on the device.
