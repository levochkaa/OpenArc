OpenArc

Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

OpenArc is an open-source browser created by Lev Poznyakov. This policy covers the OpenArc apps for Mac, iPhone and iPad, the sync and update service at api.openarc.su, and this website. Questions and requests go to support@openarc.su.

Without an account

Your Spaces, tabs, history, passwords and website data stay on your device. The apps show no ads and send no analytics or crash reports. To check for updates, the Mac app asks api.openarc.su for the latest version; like any web request, this reveals your IP address and the app's version. This website loads its list of releases from the same server.

Websites you visit, the search engine you choose and the extensions you install receive information under their own terms, as in any browser.

With a sync account

Signing in to sync is optional. When you do, our server stores:

  • How you signed in: the provider (Apple, Google, GitHub or an email code), the account ID the provider gives us, your email address and whether the provider verified it. We do not store your name or photo.
  • Your devices: the name and kind of each device you sign in on, and when it last synced.
  • What you sync: Spaces with their names, icons and order; tabs with their addresses, titles, icon addresses, custom titles, folders, pinned state, order and last use; and the records that merge changes between devices, including markers that keep deleted tabs deleted.

Browsing history, passwords, cookies and website data are not synced. Synced data travels over HTTPS but is not end-to-end encrypted: our server keeps it so that your other devices can download it.

Signing in with an email code sends your address to the email delivery service that sends the code. The code is stored hashed and expires after ten minutes. If two sign-in methods report the same verified email address, they open the same account.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, OpenArc receives your Google account ID, your email address and whether Google verified it, and uses them only to sign you in and to recognize your account. OpenArc's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who processes your data

The sync service and its database run on a server we rent from Aeza. Apple, Google and GitHub handle sign-in under their own policies, an email delivery service sends sign-in codes, and GitHub hosts app downloads. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Account and sync data stay until you ask us to delete your account. Records of sign-ins in progress and of email codes are deleted within two hours after they expire.

Your choices

You can use OpenArc without an account, and sign out at any time. To get a copy of your data or to delete your account and everything synced with it, write to support@openarc.su from the email address of your account; we complete deletions within 30 days.

Children

OpenArc's sync service is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly hold their data.

Changes

When this policy changes, this page shows the new version and its date. Changes that affect data we already hold are also announced in the release notes.
