OpenArc

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

OpenArc is an open-source browser created by Lev Poznyakov. This policy covers the OpenArc app for Mac and this website. Questions go to support@openarc.su.

Your data stays on your device

OpenArc has no accounts and no sync, and the app sends nothing to us. Your Spaces, tabs, history, passwords and website data stay on your device. We do not receive them, so we have nothing to sell, share or delete. The app shows no ads and sends no analytics or crash reports.

Websites you visit, the search engine you choose and the extensions you install receive information under their own terms, as in any browser.

Updates

To check for updates, the Mac app asks GitHub for the latest version and downloads updates from it. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address and the app's version to GitHub, under its privacy statement.

This website

These pages run on a server we rent from DigitalOcean. They set no cookies and use no analytics.

Changes

When this policy changes, this page shows the new version and its date. A change that makes the app send data to us is also announced in the release notes before it takes effect.
