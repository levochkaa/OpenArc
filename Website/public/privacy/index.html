OpenArc

Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

OpenArc is an open-source browser created by Lev Poznyakov. This policy covers the OpenArc apps for Mac, iPhone and iPad, the sync and update service at api.openarc.su, and this website. Questions and requests go to support@openarc.su.

Without an account

Your Spaces, tabs, history, passwords and website data stay on your device. The app shows no ads and sends no analytics or crash reports.

Websites you visit, the search engine you choose and the extensions you install receive information under their own terms, as in any browser.

With a sync account

Signing in to sync is optional. When you do, our server stores:

  • How you signed in: the provider (Apple, Google, GitHub or an email code), the account ID the provider gives us, your email address and whether the provider verified it. We do not store your name or photo. For Sign in with Apple, we also keep a token from Apple that lets us end OpenArc's access to your Apple Account when you delete your account.
  • Your devices: the name and kind of each device you sign in on, and when it last synced.
  • What you sync: Spaces with their names, icons and order; tabs with their addresses, titles, icon addresses, custom titles, folders, pinned state, order and last use; and the records that merge changes between devices, including markers that keep deleted tabs deleted.

Browsing history, passwords, cookies and website data are not synced. Synced data travels over HTTPS but is not end-to-end encrypted: our server keeps it so that your other devices can download it.

Signing in with an email code sends your address to the email delivery service that sends the code. The code is stored hashed and expires after ten minutes. If two sign-in methods report the same verified email address, they open the same account.

Shared links

Signed in, you can share a Space, a folder or a quote as a link. Our server then stores what you chose to share: the Space or folder name, its icon and colors, and each tab's title, address and icon; or the quoted text with the page's title, address and icon. Anyone with the link can see it, and its page names no account. Shared pages load nothing from other sites.

Your links are listed in the app's settings, where you can remove each one. A removed link stops working at once, though pages already open or cached for up to a minute may still show it. Links are deleted with your account.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, OpenArc receives your Google account ID, your email address and whether Google verified it, and uses them only to sign you in and to recognize your account. OpenArc's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Updates

To check for updates, the Mac app asks api.openarc.su for the latest version and downloads updates through it from GitHub. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address and the app's version. Choosing the Beta channel adds that choice to the request. The App Store updates the iPhone and iPad app, which does not ask our server for updates.

Ad blocker

The ad blocker is off by default. When you turn it on, the Mac app downloads its filter lists from easylist.to and secure.fanboy.co.nz, and again about once a week. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to those sites. The lists are stored on your Mac, and blocking happens there.

Who processes your data

The sync service and its database run on a server we rent from DigitalOcean. Apple, Google and GitHub handle sign-in under their own policies, an email delivery service sends sign-in codes, and GitHub hosts app downloads. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Account and sync data, and links you shared, stay until you remove them or delete your account. Records of sign-ins in progress and of email codes are deleted within two hours after they expire.

Your choices

You can use OpenArc without an account, and sign out at any time.

To delete your account, choose Delete Account in Settings › Accounts on Mac, or in Account & Sync on iPhone and iPad. Our server deletes the account and everything synced with it right away, and your other devices stop syncing. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to end OpenArc's access. Spaces, tabs and website data on your devices stay there until you remove them; the app offers to clear them when you delete the account.

To get a copy of your data, or to delete an account you can no longer sign in to, write to support@openarc.su from the email address of your account; we complete these requests within 30 days.

Children

OpenArc's sync service is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly hold their data.

This website

These pages run on the same server. They set no cookies and use no analytics.

Changes

When this policy changes, this page shows the new version and its date. Changes that affect data we already hold are also announced in the release notes.
